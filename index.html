FilmStacks Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

FilmStacks is a catalogue of the films you own on disc. It is built to work without an account, and it is deliberately built so that almost nothing about your collection ever leaves your phone. The short version:

Your collection stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud Drive. There are no accounts, no user database of ours, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no tracking and no data sale. We cannot see what films you own.

Your library

Titles, shelves, formats, editions, ratings, notes, custom fields, wishlist entries and your own cover photos are stored in a file on your device. We hold no copy and we cannot read it.

iCloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud backup, FilmStacks writes the same snapshot the manual export produces into your own private iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account, in a container reserved for this app. We operate no server for it, we hold no copy and we cannot read it. It can be turned off in Settings at any time, and deleting the app's iCloud data removes it.

Film metadata, and the one request that leaves your phone

Film titles, posters, cast, runtimes and streaming availability come from The Movie Database (TMDB). FilmStacks does not call TMDB directly. It calls a small service we run on Cloudflare, which holds the TMDB key so that it is not shipped inside the app.

That service receives, and only for as long as it takes to answer:

  • the film title you typed into search, or the film id you opened
  • the barcode digits, when you scan a disc
  • the ordinary technical details every internet request carries, such as an IP address and a timestamp

It receives no account, no name, no email, no device identifier and no advertising identifier, because the app has none to send. Nothing ties one request to another or to you. Answers from TMDB are cached by film, not by person.

The shared barcode map

Disc barcodes are not in any public film database, so FilmStacks builds the map from its users. When you confirm that a scanned barcode is a particular film, that single pairing (barcode, film) is stored so the next person who scans that disc is spared the work.

What is stored is only the pairing and a count. There is nothing attached to it that could identify you, and it says nothing about the rest of your collection. Scanning alone stores nothing; only a confirmation does. If you would rather contribute nothing, you can add titles by searching instead of scanning.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read the barcode on a case and to look at the cover so FilmStacks can tell a 4K release from a Blu-ray. All of that runs on your device, using Apple's Vision framework. Camera frames are never uploaded and never stored. If you choose your own cover photo for a title, it is saved on your device and goes nowhere else.

Exports and shared images

CSV exports, backup files and the shelf poster image are produced on your device and go only where you send them. FilmStacks uploads none of them.

Purchases

FilmStacks Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts the small service described above and processes those requests on our behalf.
  • TMDB supplies the film metadata and artwork. FilmStacks is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Apple handles purchases and, if you enable it, your iCloud Drive backup.

Deleting your data

Deleting a title removes it from the app. Deleting FilmStacks removes the on-device library entirely. If you used iCloud backup, remove that copy in Settings, Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Account Storage. A barcode confirmation you contributed cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing personal to remove from it; if you would like a specific pairing corrected, email us and we will fix it.

FilmStacks is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy
